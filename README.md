# Color Lock – Privacy Policy

Effective October 6, 2026  
App: Color Lock (com.damightyapp.colorlock)  
Publisher: [YOUR NAME OR STUDIO]

Color Lock is a tile puzzle game. This policy explains what information the app uses and who else receives it. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or contact list.

## Information stored on your device

Your progress (unlocked levels, best moves, stars, streak, hints, trophies) and your settings (theme, music, volume) are saved on your device only. We do not receive this data. Deleting the app or its data removes it.

## Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and information about your device and how you interact with ads, in order to show ads, limit how often you see them, measure results, and prevent fraud. Ads may be personalized depending on your choices.

- In regions where it is required, such as the EU and UK, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are used.
- On iOS, the app asks permission before tracking you across other companies' apps and websites.
- You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings.
- Learn more in [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

You can remove interstitial ads permanently with the "Remove ads" purchase. Optional rewarded videos are shown only when you choose to watch one.

## Purchases

If you buy "Remove ads", the payment is handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not see or store your payment details. We only learn that the purchase was completed so we can turn ads off for you.

## Online leaderboard

If a version of the game offers an online leaderboard, it shows your total stars, daily-puzzle result, and streak next to your display name. This feature is optional and may not be available in the mobile app.

## Children

Color Lock is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data, or to object to its use. Because we do not hold personal data about you ourselves, most requests are best directed to Google through your Google account's ad settings. You can also contact us with any question.
